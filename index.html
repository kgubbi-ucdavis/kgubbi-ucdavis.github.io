About Me

Researcher, engineer, and builder at the intersection of hardware security and AI

I'm the Co-Founder and CTO of ChipSage Labs, where I work on AI for chip design and design verification. My doctoral research in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC Davis focuses on hardware security, VLSI design, and machine learning for EDA, including LLM-assisted RTL verification, hardware fuzzing, and secure ASIC design.

I've interned at AMD and Samsung Austin Research Center (SARC), where I worked on ML/RL-based optimization for chip design and filed a patent. I also lead award-winning research teams and contribute to hardware security initiatives.
